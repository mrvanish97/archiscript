# 0.3.0: independent VDP tensor

Status: implemented in the Lean library. This release adds the concrete
symmetric monoidal structure described in the sibling
[monoidal VDP review](../../archiscript-docs/chapter-1/stage-2/archiscript_monoidal_vdp_review.tex).

`Partition.tensor P Q` classifies the full product carrier. Its member indices
are the full product of the two finite member sets, with coverage and
inhabitance inherited from the factors. `SemanticPartition.tensor` combines
independently stated meanings and their `HasMembers` proofs. `Partition.unit`
is the one-member Unit VDP. `Operation.tensor` combines independent partial
member maps; it is defined only where both factors are defined.

The public `Monoidal` module provides classification-preserving associator,
left and right unitor, and symmetry isomorphisms. They induce invertible member
operations. Lean checks tensor identity and interchange, naturality of the
structural operations, pentagon, triangle, involutive symmetry, and the
symmetric hexagon.

Tensor never prunes a member pair, collapses a self tensor into one carrier
coordinate, or models same-value synchronization, common refinement,
resource effects, or runtime parallelism. Joint architectural relevance and
possible concurrency are review questions. Lean checks the declared model;
human review judges its relation to the system being designed.

The [monoidal example](../ArchiScriptExamples/Monoidal.lean),
[regression checks](../ArchiScriptTests/Monoidal.lean), and
[skill smoke example](../skills/archiscript/examples/CurrentApi.lean) exercise
these laws. General consumer/coarsening analysis and resource/effect semantics
remain deferred in [future-coarsening.md](future-coarsening.md) and later design
work.
