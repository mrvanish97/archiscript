# Next-version backlog after the 0.7 semantic rewrite

## Whole-inventory orphan/SCC diagnostics and coverage accounting

Inspect the entire declared Node/VDP and Generator/Operation inventory alongside
Causes. Detect isolated nodes, unused declarations and rootless SCCs, including
components absent from every Cause-starting traversal. Distinguish canonical
rooted production from nominal ownership, cyclic markers, empty supplier images
and unobservable status. Report intentional exclusions/dead declarations
separately from unexplained orphans; retain author dispositions.

Prioritize a useful diagnostic when feasible, with the accepted counterexamples
in `SourceCoverage.sourceOnly` and `UngroundedCycle.certified` as regressions.
Also cover an unrelated owned SCC obstructing an observable Cone's structural
boundary proof. State the diagnostic's assurance scope; it is not by itself a
global semantic coverage theorem. In 0.7.0 this remains manual author review,
with explicit assumptions in model handoffs.

## Automatic all-generated-Expression/Cone coverage

**0.7 status: NOT AUTOMATICALLY VERIFIED.**

Prove that every finite linear Expression generated from the closed primitive
inventory, including paths beginning inside cycles and justified tensor imports,
is covered by at least one declared one-Cause Cone. The current certificate
quantifies over derived local contexts and checks complete terminal boundaries;
it does not prove this additional universal union-of-Cones property.

This stronger obligation is separate from inventory diagnostics: a traversal of
only Cause-generated Expressions can miss a rootless component entirely. Neither
check substitutes for the other. A walk over nominal VDP values is insufficient:
equal denotations do not identify
occurrences, and recursive cross-Cone supplier derivations retain histories.
A candidate symbolic checker needs a precise `GeneratedExpression`/`InCone`
relation, induction/coverage proof, disconnected-cycle and omitted-generator
negative cases, and a sound connection to the existing root-instance semantics.
Real-world inventory completeness remains an author assumption even after this
relative theorem is proved. Do not replace it with a selected family or list.

## Coproduct semantic-use discipline and guarded architectural alternatives

**0.7.0 status: coproduct algebra is proved; the intended architectural use
of every coproduct is NOT AUTOMATICALLY VERIFIED.** See the
[FAQ on alternatives versus independent supplier branches](../README.md#can-a-coproduct-combine-two-independent-supplier-branches-into-one-alternative-response)
and the matching author/reviewer rule in
[`skills/archiscript/SKILL.md`](../skills/archiscript/SKILL.md).

For 0.8.0, investigate a **precisely scoped, enforceable guard** against
misusing a coproduct as an implicit logical OR, arbitration mechanism or
hidden branch between independently produced Operations. Preserve
`Partition.coproduct` as the *tagged-sum carrier/member construction*
and preserve legitimate injections, `copair` and coproduct algebra on
already tagged semantic inputs. Do **not** turn coproduct into an
`Architecture.Inputs` constructor that silently merges separate
suppliers, and do not prohibit valid alternative input or output types.

Concrete acceptance cases and proposed obligations:

- **Allowed declared boundary alternatives:** one input whose carrier
  is `Sum Nat String` (a request is a number *or* a string), or one
  output whose carrier is `Sum SuccessPayload ErrorPayload`
  (a response is success *or* error), with justified selected Subdomains
  and the existing finite, inhabited, disjoint, covering VDP proofs.
  A coproduct's coverage theorem is **relative to its declared sum
  carrier**; a real API's completeness remains an external assumption.
- **Allowed tagged handling:** an Operation maps a semantically
  distinguished input member to a defined `Sum.inl` or `Sum.inr`
  result, or `copair` consumes an *already tagged* alternative.
  Its source distinctions, tag provenance and partiality must remain
  explicit; no new nondeterministic choice is inferred.
- **Rejected implicit sum join:** two independent producers or
  Operations, one reaching a success-shaped VDP and one an
  error-shaped VDP, are not automatically mutually exclusive and
  cannot become one selected response by a coproduct alone.
  A common Effect tensor is not an implicit choice; an actual
  response-producing Operation and justified supplier route are
  still required.
- **Separate tensor regression:** `Request ⊗ ServiceStatus` can feed
  one response VDP with a success/error coproduct carrier.
  `(Invalid, Available)` and `(Invalid, Unavailable)` may both map to
  `InvalidInput`, but that proves **value-level output invariance**,
  not absence of the status supplier or execution ordering.
  Keep P/E verification and defined output mapping distinct.

Candidate 0.8.0 deliverable: define an audit/checkable declaration or
restricted architectural surface that records **where** a sum is used
(as a boundary/VDP alternative versus claimed producer combination),
**which evidence identifies the tag**, and **which declared Operation
actually produces a tagged result**. Add small positive and negative
Lean/regression examples for the cases above, and ensure any rejection
diagnostic cites the precise checked rule. Before claiming automatic
enforcement, state and prove the actual soundness/scope of the guard;
avoid a syntactic ban that rejects valid `copair` or legitimate
`Sum`-typed carrier choices.

**Limit:** neither a generic Lean theorem nor syntactic inspection can
establish that the architect's chosen `Sum` carrier truthfully represents
all real-world possibilities or that a deployed implementation obeys it.
If 0.8.0 can enforce only the structural part, report the remaining
semantic-intent and external-adequacy checks as **MANUAL / NOT
AUTOMATICALLY VERIFIED**. Do not silently promote an audit convention
into a full architecture-correctness guarantee.

## Optional analysis improvements

- Derive maintainable finite exact abstractions for larger provenance-sensitive
  fragments. Prove their query agreement; do not infer finite state from finite
  VDP members. The current bounded saturation returns an exact result only once
  closure is checked. SCC/worklist optimization needs a preservation proof.
- Provide ergonomic proof combinators for universal context and terminal
  occurrence obligations without removing required proof fields.
- Profile provenance-cache closure and catalog lookup on at least two realistic
  inventory sizes before choosing an index or worklist optimization. Repeated
  full-list scans are a possible cost, not a measured slowdown. Any replacement
  must preserve normalized histories, source positions, closure checks and proof
  coverage; retain small semantic regressions independently of benchmark sizes.
- Improve witnessed diagnostics for nondecidable semantic contracts while
  preserving the distinction between an unsolved proof and an invalid model.
- Explore a frontend only after the semantic API is reviewed. No Markdown parser,
  generated theorem language, scheduler, resource calculus or implementation
  conformance claim is implied by this backlog.

The 0.7 shared-target correction is implemented, not deferred here: finite
normalized-history bases require seeds, full-source closure and realizations;
shared suffixes retain distinct histories. Future analysis work may broaden
that supported fragment to more general cyclic
observation quotients, with soundness/completeness proofs. None of that work
reintroduces named unique VDP uses, cloned graphs or generator-output uniqueness.

The 0.7 input-source and no-observation cases are also implemented: each declared
generator has a mandatory source-accounting obligation, and each Cause has an
observable or explicitly unobservable status. Infinite root-instance types do
not require infinite caches: only each concrete Cause's canonical origin is
supported. Composition of repeated external invocations would be a separate
future extension, with its own semantics.
